Why

A blind spot and a clean result are the same reading

High is awarded for a clean picture — one component clearly worst, nothing moving before it. But a clean picture is also exactly what you see when the fault is invisible to the instrument you're holding.

A quarter of the real causes are network faults, which live in span latency. We were reading metrics.

2:15 — say "A confidence score measured inside one instrument cannot detect the failure 'we are using the wrong instrument.' That's the transferable line. It's true of a blood test, a survey, and an attribution model."
